Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CJP RUNNER (the "Game"), published by MAS PERSIAN ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the Game on an Android device. We designed the Game to collect as little information as possible. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

Information we do not collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise location. We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (for example your coin balance, best score and sound setting) in the Game's local storage on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.

We do not control how Google handles this information. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Other services

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services in the Game. If we add any such service in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Sharing and retention

We do not share personal information with third parties, other than the data collected by the advertising provider described above. Because the data we store lives only on your device, you control how long it is kept.
